About Kedimz Adventure

Kedimz Adventure is a side-scrolling platformer about an orange cat with a red scarf. Run, jump, bop blobs and collect coins across five worlds, each ending in a boss.

Damage is forgiving: a hit makes a big Kedimz small first, pits cost a heart rather than the level, and checkpoints refill your hearts. Hidden Kitty Gems, secret pipes and a shop with upgrades reward exploring.

3 quick tips

  1. Stomp on enemies or paw swipe them, but jump over spiky ones or use a fire or ice shot.
  2. Every 100 coins gives an extra life.
  3. A pipe with a sign pointing down leads to a secret room. Go down it to look for gems.
